Privacy policy

Last updated October 10, 2026

odo is a dream journal. Your dreams are personal, so we collect as little as we can and never sell anything about you.

What we store

  • Your account: email address, the name you choose, your language setting, and when you joined.
  • Your dreams: the words of each dream, the date, and the dreamscape made from it (title, scenes, pictures, reading, and the people, places and feelings in it).
  • Usage: how many dreamscapes you made today, so we can keep the service fair.

If you use odo without an account, your dreams stay on your device and never reach our servers.

Your voice

When you speak a dream, your device's speech recognition (Apple's, on iPhone) turns it into text. We never receive or store audio.

Who processes your data

  • Supabase hosts our database and sign-in (United States).
  • OpenAI composes your dreamscapes, paints their pictures and writes each reading. When you make one, the text of that dream is sent to OpenAI's API, which processes it to return the scenes, images and reading and does not use API data to train its models. Anthropic's API is used as a backup composer if OpenAI is unavailable. We ask before the first time this happens.

What we don't do

No ads. No tracking across other apps or websites. No selling or sharing your data with data brokers. No one at odo reads your dreams except to fix a problem you ask us to look at.

Deleting your data

Delete any dream from the app at any time. To delete everything, open Profile and tap Delete account. This permanently removes your account and every dream in it.

Children

odo is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.
